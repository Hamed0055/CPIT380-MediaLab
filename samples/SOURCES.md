# Asset sources and rights

## Public-domain sample photographs

- `images/09_nasa_astronaut.jpg`: NASA, photograph of astronaut Eileen Collins. Distributed in scikit-image example data. Original: https://flic.kr/p/r9qvLn . Documentation: https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.astronaut . Re-encoded as JPEG for this pack.
- `images/10_nasa_hubble.jpg`: NASA/HubbleSite, Hubble eXtreme Deep Field, distributed in scikit-image example data. Documentation: https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.hubble_deep_field . Re-encoded as JPEG for this pack.

Both are identified as public domain by scikit-image documentation. NASA media use guidelines (including restrictions on endorsement, insignia and commercial use of identifiable people): https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/ .

## Generated assets

All other media and texts are original test assets generated for this pack with Python/Pillow/NumPy, FFmpeg and generic eSpeak speech synthesis. No third-party songs, stock clips, or copied prose are included. No font files or tool binaries are distributed.
